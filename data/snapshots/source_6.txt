NASSCOM-Zinnov India GCC Landscape Report 2026.
Published count of Global Capability Centres in India reached 2,117 operating units as of August 2026 across major tech hubs.
Bengaluru accounts for 36% of active centers, followed by Hyderabad and Pune.
Average year-one benchmark cost for a 50-person setup in Bengaluru ranges from US$1.8M to US$2.2M, with senior engineer salaries between 22 and 32 lakh rupees.
Captive centers continue transitioning from cost arbitrage to strategic engineering and research delivery.
