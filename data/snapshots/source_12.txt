Government of Karnataka - Karnataka GCC Policy 2024-2029.
The policy provides targeted incentives for Global Capability Centres establishing operations in Bengaluru and Beyond Bengaluru clusters (Mysuru, Mangaluru, Hubballi-Dharwad).
Incentive provisions include capital support fractions for high-value equipment, employment-linked subsidies for local talent creation, power tariff rebates, and stamp duty concessions.
Dedicated nodal facilitation is delivered through Invest Karnataka and the Department of Electronics, IT, Bt and S&T.
Official Portal: https://investkarnataka.co.in.
