Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) - Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.
Rules and implementing provisions under the DPDP Act are rolling out phased compliance mandates for data fiduciaries.
Mandates include reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breach, timely breach notifications, consent architectures, and internal grievance redressal mechanisms.
Incident reporting rules mandate reporting within specified windows to CERT-In and relevant authorities.
Official Portal: https://www.meity.gov.in.
