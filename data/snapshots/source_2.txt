Ministry of Corporate Affairs - SPICe+ Integrated Incorporation.
Private limited company incorporation requires at least two directors, with at least one resident in India for 182 days or more.
Integrated SPICe+ form INC-32 covers DIN allotment, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, GST registration, and bank account opening.
Post-incorporation filings include INC-22 registered office verification within 30 days, ADT-1 auditor appointment within 30 days, and INC-20A commencement declaration within 180 days.
Official Portal: https://www.mca.gov.in.
